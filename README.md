# SAT Prep Bytes Support

**SAT Prep Bytes** is a free, bite-sized SAT prep app for iPhone and iPad. Personalized study plans, 2,700+ real SAT-format questions, and instant explanations — no ads, no subscriptions.

## Getting Started

1. Download SAT Prep Bytes from the App Store
2. Tap **Get Started** on the welcome screen
3. Enter your name, grade, target SAT score, and test date
4. Complete the **Diagnostic Test** (20 questions)
5. Your personalized study plan is ready — tap **Start My Study Plan**

## Features

- **Diagnostic Test** — 20-question assessment that maps your strengths and weaknesses
- **Topic Drills** — bite-sized practice sessions focused on one skill at a time
- **Full Practice Tests** — timed, scored on the 400–1600 scale, works offline
- **SAT Vocabulary** — high-frequency SAT words in quick quiz format
- **Dashboard** — streak, estimated score, days until your SAT, and your next recommended session
- **Progress** — score trend, accuracy by topic, and full session history
- **Review** — go back to every question you got wrong, organized by topic

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the app really free?**
Yes. SAT Prep Bytes is completely free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. All questions are stored on your device. You can practice anywhere with no Wi-Fi needed.

**What SAT topics are covered?**
Math: Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem Solving & Data Analysis, Geometry & Trigonometry.
Reading & Writing: Information & Ideas, Craft & Structure, Expression of Ideas, Standard English Conventions, SAT Vocabulary.

**How is my estimated score calculated?**
Your estimated score is calculated based on your accuracy across all topics, weighted by how each topic appears on the real Digital SAT.

**How do I reset my profile or start over?**
Go to the **Progress** tab → tap the ⚙️ settings icon in the top right → select **Reset Profile**.

**My questions aren't loading.**
Make sure you have opened the app at least once with an internet connection so questions can download. After that, everything works offline.

## Privacy Policy

SAT Prep Bytes takes your privacy seriously.

**What we collect:**
- Name, grade, target score, and test date — entered by you, stored only on your device
- Practice session data (questions answered, scores, streaks) — stored only on your device

**What we do NOT collect:**
- No account required
- No email address required
- No location data
- No advertising identifiers
- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No data is transmitted to any server

All data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.
